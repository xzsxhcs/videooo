==================================================
1. CREATIVE OBJECTIVE
==================================================

Create a 35.000-second dark experimental motion film about the difference between sending a signal and receiving confirmation.

The film depicts:

a nearly invisible night-sea horizon,

a small distant sender point on that horizon,

and a sequence of repeated pulses traveling along the horizon toward the right side of the frame.

The sender behaves consistently.

Subtle events begin appearing at the far-right end of the horizon.

These events gradually suggest that something may be responding.

The viewer learns the timing of the sender.

Eventually the viewer begins waiting for the next transmission.

At 30.0 seconds, the expected sixth normal pulse does not occur.

Later, after a period of stillness and silence, one final extremely weak ghost pulse is transmitted.

Nothing is confirmed.

The final feeling should be:

quiet,
unresolved,
slightly oppressive,
and ambiguous.

The viewer should not know whether:

nothing responded,

or

the response was simply too weak to recognize.

This is not a literal science-fiction narrative.

Do not introduce characters, spaceships, antennas, machinery, interface graphics, or explanatory story elements.

==================================================
56. FIDELITY CORRECTION REQUIREMENT
==================================================

If the first full render differs materially from the approved design:

FIX IT.

Examples:

If E1 reads too strongly:
reduce it.

If E2 feels like an obvious response:
reduce it.

If E3 looks like a definite answer:
make it more ambiguous.

If the camera move is noticeable as a camera move:
reduce its perceptual prominence while keeping the approved 1.000→1.030 transform.

If the 30.0 s absence does not register:
do NOT add an effect.
Instead verify that P1–P5 have taught the 4.8-second pattern clearly enough.

If the ghost pulse feels like a climax:
reduce it.

If the soundtrack feels musical:
remove musical qualities.

If the G5 tone sounds like conventional sonar:
refine the timbre/reverb while preserving the approved pitch and timing.

If the background becomes visually dominant:
darken or soften it.

If the text becomes a graphic focal point:
reduce its visual prominence while preserving the approved typography specification.

If silence is not actually silent:
correct the audio.

After corrections:

render the complete film again.

Repeat the audit until no substantial mismatch remains.

==================================================
58. FINAL EMOTIONAL TARGET
==================================================

When the screen reaches black, the viewer should feel a quiet pressure with no clear exit.

The film must not prove:

"No one answered."

It should only prove:

"No confirmation arrived."

The audience should remain uncertain whether:

nothing responded,

or

something did respond,

but too weakly to become certainty.

The final ghost pulse means:

the sender has not received confirmation,

but has not entirely stopped trying.

Do not resolve that ambiguity.
